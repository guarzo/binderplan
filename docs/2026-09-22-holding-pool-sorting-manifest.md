1. **EDGE Watches, page 1:** Unease (2) and Environmental Momentum (4), leaving three deliberate empty pockets.
2. **Existing-theme EDGE, page 1:** five candidates, ordered Volume II then Volume I; four empty pockets.
3. **REDUNDANT, pages 1–3:** grouped by challenged theme, not species.
4. **FUTURE SELF, page 1:** one card; leave the remaining pockets open rather than filling them.
5. **Event Staging:** one empty page at the back.

This uses seven nine-pocket pages while preserving section boundaries.
