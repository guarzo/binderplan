# Emolga Masterset published-gallery evidence (received/reviewed 2026-09-24)

These thirteen byte-for-byte copies were archived from the existing published `static/images/binder/emolga-masterset/` gallery before card crops or inventory edits. The eleven `emolga_N.webp` images show the owner-approved 2×2 pages, but they are **existing published photos, not camera originals**. Their resolution, cropping, glare, sleeves and compression limit identification of printing, set, numbering and variants. The file names preserve the published source names. `SHA256SUMS` records the hashes of these archived bytes; each archive file was compared byte-for-byte against its published source and the checksum list verified with `sha256sum -c` on 2026-09-24.

`Missing Emolga BW-9 81.jpg` and `Missing Emolga 025 BW-P.jpg` are existing published *reference images* for wanted 081/BW-P (page 1 position 3) and 025/BW-P (page 2 position 1). They do **not** evidence possession. Only 42 other page pockets are provisionally treated as physical cards. No camera originals or independent proof of authenticity, exact printing, or ownership beyond these photos were supplied. The archived copies are evidence; card crops are derived assets and not scans.

On 2026-09-24, the visible card numbers and artwork were cross-referenced against Bulbapedia's [Emolga (TCG) card list](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Emolga_(TCG)) for set associations. Direct article retrieval returned HTTP 403, so the initial cross-reference used indexed card-list text; the owner then supplied the full English/Japanese expansion, number, and rarity table in the review conversation, confirming those matches. The table is a catalogue of possible printings, **not** evidence that its other entries are present in these pages or newly wanted. Magnifying the archived photo footers corrected page 2 bottom-right to 49/99 (not 49/98), page 5 bottom-left to 019/050 (not 019/059), page 7 top-left to 054/195 (not 059/195), and page 8 bottom-left to 112/086 (not 077/086); page 9 bottom-right 062/060 was also checked. Catalog associations plus the published photos do not establish exact variants, finishes, authenticity or in-hand identity.
