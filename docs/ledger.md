# Curatorial ledger

An append-only record of **contested** placement decisions and **every** release.

This is not an inventory. It does not track where each card physically is, and it says nothing about cards whose placement was never in doubt. It exists to answer one question: *why does this card sit where it sits?* — so that periodic audits under `CURATORIAL_AUDIT_PROMPT.md` do not re-litigate calls that were already argued out.

## How to use this file

**Append only.** Entries are never edited or deleted. When a later audit overturns an earlier call, write a **new** entry that cites the old one — `Elemental Solitude → Legendary Bearing (reverses 2026-08-01)`. The reversal history is the most useful thing in this file; editing in place destroys it.

**What earns an entry**

- A contested call — two or more themes genuinely competed for the card
- A correction — an earlier read was wrong and the card moved
- Every release, contested or not. Letting a card go is the one action that cannot be undone.
- Theme proposals, accepted or rejected, with the §8 test that decided it

A card that obviously belonged where it went gets no entry.

**Vocabulary.** Destinations use names that already exist — Volume 1 and Volume 2 sub-themes, and the holding-box sections from `content/guides/holding-box.md`: EDGE, REDUNDANT, HERITAGE, FUTURE SELF, RELEASE. No parallel naming scheme.

**Citations.** `§` references are to `CURATORIAL_AUDIT_PROMPT.md`.

**No backfill.** This ledger starts 2026-08-01. Moves made before that date are not recorded, because there is no reliable record of them and reconstructing one from memory would put fiction into an audit trail.

## 2026-08-01 — Holding box sort, images 54–60

Governing doc: `CURATORIAL_AUDIT_PROMPT.md` §7D (Reserve Card Analysis).
Full analysis: [`holding-box-placement.md`](holding-box-placement.md).

84 loose cards assessed. 75 placed, 4 held to EDGE, 3 released, 1 duplicate, 1 to the Emolga Masterset. Of those, the decisions below were contested or corrected.

### Corrections under §5A — contained power vs. gathering power

§5A: Legendary Bearing is power **contained and static**; Awakened Power is **gathering, before release**. Active attack energy disqualifies both. Five cards were initially placed against this rule.

#### Latios — EN 110HP
**Legendary Bearing → Elemental Solitude**
Legendary Bearing requires stillness with gravity; this is a body in motion. §5A also calls out "legendary by name only" as insufficient, which was doing unacknowledged work in the first read. Placed on what the art shows: one Pokemon alone in an element that fills the frame.

#### Groudon ex — グラードンex, JP
**Legendary Bearing → On the Attack**
Rearing out of molten rock with the landscape deforming around it is active energy. That disqualifies Legendary Bearing (power must be contained) and Awakened Power (this is release, not gathering), leaving On the Attack.

#### Charizard VSTAR — EN 280HP
**Awakened Power → On the Attack**
The flame is being thrown, not gathered. Awakened Power is specifically the moment *before* inevitability.

#### Torterra Lv.X — ドダイトス Lv.X, JP promo
**Awakened Power → On the Attack**
Bursting upward with the landscape already in motion is release, not the tension before it.

#### Blastoise VMAX — 水箭龜VMAX, Chinese
**Awakened Power → Legendary Bearing**
Fully realized static power, which §5A rules out for Awakened Power. The cannons are trained but unfired — contained power presented as an object of awe. Judgment call: Intimidation was the alternative and was rejected only because that theme was already overloaded. Worth revisiting if Legendary Bearing ever crowds.

### Corrections under §5B — age is not a placement argument

§5B retired era identity. Two cards had been placed on how old they were rather than what they show.

#### Pikachu — EN vintage 60HP "Growl"
**Enduring Presence → Calm in Nature**
The original reasoning was that the visibly early, rounded character design "reads as a preserved earlier version." That is an age argument wearing a visual costume, and §5B forbids it. Judged on signal alone: a small Pokemon standing at ease on open pebbled ground.

#### Aerodactyl — プテラ, JP No.142
**Enduring Presence → Legendary Bearing**
"Revived fossil carried out of deep time" is lore and age, not visual signal — and §4 excludes attack poses from Enduring Presence in any case. What the art shows is a complete, unopposed form holding the sky over an empty primeval landscape: contained power, timeless bearing.

#### Gastly — EN Base Set 33/102
**Intimidation (unchanged) — alternative struck**
Placement stands. Recording this because the discarded alternative was "Enduring Presence, as a vintage form," which is the same §5B error as the two above. Vintage is a print fact, not a depiction.

### Rebalancing under §1 — no theme may be a dumping ground

Intimidation initially drew 11 of the 84 cards — four Houndour, three Gengar-line, plus Gastly, Genesect, Golem 4 and Darkrai. A full nine-pocket page of mostly dark-type, dark-background art reads as monotonous rather than menacing, and §1 makes that structural rather than cosmetic. Two cards moved; Intimidation settled at 9 and Contemplation rose from 3 to 5.

#### Genesect — ゲノセクト, JP BW
**Intimidation → Contemplation**
Poised low over a night cityscape with the cannon carried, not fired. Reads as watchful rather than threatening.

#### Houndour Lv.13 — EN, HGSS-era reverse holo
**Intimidation → Contemplation**
Alert on a stone outcrop, head level, watching something out of frame. Stillness and attention, not menace.

Alternatives considered and not needed: splitting Intimidation into two spreads (the held stare vs. the bared teeth), and holding Gengar-line cards back for a later rotation. Both remain available if the theme grows again.

### Releases

§7D permits Place / Hold / Retire; §1 prefers subtraction to forced inclusion. All three were contested before being let go.

#### Pokemon Communication — EN trainer
**Reserve → RELEASE**
The art depicts a device with a Pikachu inside it. There is no Pokemon-in-a-world scene to place, so no volume sub-theme can hold it. Touchstones was considered and rejected: it would have been an era-identity argument of the kind §5B retires.

#### Ditto — EN, e-Card era 60HP
**Reserve → RELEASE**
Fists up on flat dark ground. No environment, no relationship, no interior state. Considered for the proposed Concealment theme and rejected there too — it is not hiding, it is posing.

#### Piplup Lv.9 — EN, DP 60HP
**Reserve → RELEASE**
Standing on grey rock in flat daylight; the weakest art in the box. Calm in Nature would have taken it as filler. The alternative considered was pairing it with the JP Piplup so the stronger card carried it — rejected as precisely the forced inclusion §1 rules out.

### Held to EDGE

#### Kecleon — カクレオン, JP promo · Ditto — メタモン, JP SV 122/165
**Reserve → EDGE**
Both genuinely depict concealment, which no current theme covers. Calm in Nature would take the Kecleon, but placing it there would waste the clearest example of an idea the binder cannot yet express. Held together pending the theme decision below. Personal Significance has a competing claim on the Kecleon — it already holds a JP Kecleon — and remains the fallback if EDGE stalls.

#### N — JP promo 180/BW-P · N — EN full art
**Reserve → EDGE**
Trainer character art, so no volume sub-theme applies. Trainer Full Arts is restricted to female Japanese trainers, closing that section on gender for both and on language for the English copy. Strong illustrations with nowhere to go; release is defensible but premature. Revisit only if a male-trainer section is ever opened.

### Theme decision — Concealment: proposed, rejected

**Proposed:** a Volume 2 theme for Pokemon whose art is about not being seen — disguised, camouflaged, hidden among look-alikes, or withheld from the viewer.

**Tested against §8.** Passes tests 1–4: it is a distinct axis (Contemplation is stillness directed outward; this is stillness directed at not being found), it has clear inclusion and exclusion rules, and it resolves two cards with no honest home. **Fails test 5** — two qualifying cards cannot sustain multiple pages.

**Rejected**, per §8's instruction to recommend against any theme failing any test. With two cards it would become the dumping ground §1 forbids: anything shadowed or partly obscured would drift in, and within a year it would be indistinguishable from Contemplation.

**Revisit when EDGE holds six or more genuine candidates.** Likely sources: Zorua, Sableye, Wobbuffet, Mimikyu.

## Open questions carried forward

- **The Trainer Full Arts rule is undocumented.** Neither `content/gallery/waifu/_index.md` ("Japanese trainer cards with expressive character art") nor §6 ("Full-art Japanese trainer supporter cards, collected purely for illustration quality") states that the section is female trainers only. The rule is real and was applied here. It should be written into §6 at minimum, since that is the governing document — otherwise every future sort re-discovers the ambiguity.
- **The reserve pool skews heavily to Volume 1.** Of 84 cards, 71 landed in Volume 1 and 4 in Volume 2. §2 makes Volume 1 structurally closed and Volume 2 the active curation area, so a pool this lopsided does not help where the work is. Volume 2 needs domestic scale, bonds, liminal edges, and self-contained stillness.
- **On the Attack sits at 10** after the §5A corrections moved three cards into it. That is the rule working as intended, but the resulting spread should be checked for undifferentiated impact.
- **Set attributions are photograph-derived.** Several cards in this session were identified through sleeves, price tags, and glare. Names and languages are reliable; set and number attributions marked *(uncertain)* in the placement plan need confirmation against the physical cards.

## 2026-08-01 (second session) — Full binder constraint; supersedes the 2026-08-01 entry above

Governing doc: `CURATORIAL_AUDIT_PROMPT.md` §2 (Volume 1 structurally closed).
Full analysis: [`holding-box-placement.md`](holding-box-placement.md), rewritten.

**This session reverses most of the session above it.** That entry recorded destinations as if the
binder had free pockets. It does not: Volume 1 is full, and Volume 2's existing themes are full
except for a single empty pocket in Quiet Familiarity page 2. §2 states this plainly — "Volume 1
is structurally closed... extracted cards must be replaced with a stronger candidate from the same
theme, or permanently retired" — and the first pass quoted §2 for an unrelated rule while ignoring
its central constraint.

Under a full binder a placement is a **challenge**, not an addition. All 75 placements recorded
above are void as instructions. Re-run against the actual spreads: 28 challengers tested, 11 won.

### Method change

Outcomes are now Swap / Redundant / Retire, using `content/guides/holding-box.md` vocabulary.
The Retire criterion is new and was added at the curator's direction: a card the binder already
owns in better form has no path to inclusion, so filing it as REDUNDANT ("keep in case taste
shifts") is self-deception. It goes to RELEASE.

### Reversals of specific entries above

- **Kecleon and Ditto SV** — recorded above as EDGE holds pending a Concealment theme. Both are
  duplicates of incumbents (Kecleon in Threshold, a Ditto-as-Pikachu in Quiet Familiarity p2).
  Now released.
- **Genesect and Houndour Lv.13 HGSS** — recorded above as moves into Contemplation to relieve
  Intimidation overload. Contemplation is full of stronger stillness-and-attention cards; both are
  redundant there. The move is void. The overload it addressed was also an artefact of the
  additive framing — there was never room for eleven cards in Intimidation.
- **The N full arts** — recorded above as having no home because Trainer Full Arts is
  female-Japanese-only. Half wrong: Legendary Bearing p2 contains **N's Plan**, and trainer cards
  appear throughout both volumes. They are volume-eligible and need assessing on artwork. Still
  held.
- **Aerodactyl → Legendary Bearing** and the other §5A/§5B corrections above stand as *reasoning*,
  but none of them are executable placements; all those cards are now redundant or retired.

### Theme decision — Concealment: rejected outright

Previously parked at "revisit when EDGE holds six candidates." Now rejected on §8 test 4 as well
as test 5. The binder already absorbs concealment cards into existing themes, so there is no
unhoused population to serve; and both candidate cards turned out to be duplicates of those very
incumbents, taking the candidate count to zero. A Concealment page could only reach viability by
stripping working cards out of Quiet Familiarity and Threshold. Do not revisit without a genuinely
unhoused cluster of six or more.

### New observation — species duplication is the real source of free pockets

Umbreon ×3, Mewtwo ×4, Charmander ×3, Lugia ×2, Latios ×2, Groudon ×2, Yveltal EX ×2, Spheal ×2,
Ursaring ×2, Typhlosion ×2. Eight of the eleven evictions above exploit a duplicate. A dedicated
audit pass on cross-theme duplication would likely free more pockets than this entire holding box
produced, and is worth scheduling ahead of the next buying trip.
