# Holding-box placement plan

Source: `tmp/converted/54_IMG_6817.webp` – `60_IMG_6823.webp` (7 photos, ~84 loose cards laid out flat). Images 01–53 are already-published binder pages and are not covered here.

Placements below are argued from **what the artwork depicts**, per the binder's organizing principle. Rarity, print run, and play value are ignored except where a card belongs to a slab section that is explicitly about release context (Touchstones, Stamped Cards).

**Governing document.** This is a §7D *Reserve Card Analysis* under `CURATORIAL_AUDIT_PROMPT.md`. Its rules are applied throughout, in particular:

- §7D outcomes are **Place / Hold / Retire** — "retire" is a real option and is used below. §1: *subtraction is preferable to forced inclusion.*
- §5A: Legendary Bearing = power **contained and static**; Awakened Power = **gathering, before release**. Active attack energy disqualifies both.
- §5B: era identity is retired. **A card's age is not an argument for its placement** — including for Enduring Presence.
- §2: new themes live in **Volume 2**, never Volume 1.
- §8: new themes must pass all five gatekeeping tests before being proposed.

**Identification caveat:** set codes were read off photographs and several are partially obscured by sleeves, shop price tags, or glare. Card names and languages are reliable; set/number attributions marked *(uncertain)* should be confirmed against the physical card before anything is published.

Destination shorthand:

- **V1** = Volume I — Lived Experience (Calm in Nature · World of People · At Rest · Joyful Action · Awakened Power · Legendary Bearing · Intimidation · On the Attack · Elemental Solitude · Contemplation)
- **V2** = Volume II — Memory, Presence, Passage (Companions · Quiet Familiarity · Enduring Presence · Threshold)
- Slab sections: Touchstones, Gold Stars, Masaki, Definitive Pokemon, Personal Significance, Stamped Cards, Trainer Full Arts
- **Emolga** = Emolga Masterset

## Flags

### 1. Cards that don't fit — Hold vs. Retire

**Note on the Trainer Full Arts rule.** Neither `content/gallery/waifu/_index.md` ("Japanese trainer cards with expressive character art") nor `CURATORIAL_AUDIT_PROMPT.md` §6 ("Full-art Japanese trainer supporter cards, collected purely for illustration quality") says *female*. The rule you applied is real but undocumented in both places. Worth writing into §6 at minimum, since that's the governing document.

### 1b. New theme proposal — Concealment (§8 gatekeeping)

Volume 2 only, per §2. Tested against all five §8 criteria:

| Test | Assessment |
|---|---|
| 1. Distinct emotional/visual axis | **Pass.** Present-but-unseen is not covered anywhere. Contemplation is stillness *directed outward*; this is stillness *directed at not being found*. |
| 2. Clear inclusion rule | **Pass.** The composition must make concealment its subject — the Pokemon is disguised, camouflaged, hidden among look-alikes, or partially withheld from the viewer. |
| 3. Clear exclusion rule | **Pass.** Merely being in shadow, small, or partly cropped does not qualify. Ghosts emerging *toward* the viewer are Intimidation, not Concealment. |
| 4. Solves a real classification problem | **Pass, narrowly.** It resolves Kecleon and SV Ditto, which currently have no honest home. Two cards is a thin problem. |
| 5. Can sustain multiple pages long-term | **Not yet demonstrated.** This is the failing test today. |

**Cannibalization:** it would take a small amount from Contemplation (already thin at three cards from this box) and from Quiet Familiarity in the Zorua/Mimikyu direction. Neither would be gutted.

**Why not a sub-theme:** under Chapter III (Passage) it would be a poor fit — Threshold is about crossing between spaces, Concealment is about refusing to occupy one visibly. It would need its own chapter, which raises the bar further.

**Risks if added prematurely:** with two qualifying cards it would become the dumping ground §1 forbids — anything shadowed or partly obscured would drift into it, and within a year it would be indistinguishable from Contemplation.

**Recommendation: do not add yet.** §8 says a theme failing any test should be recommended against, and test 5 fails. Hold Kecleon and the SV Ditto together in the box and revisit when there are six or more genuine candidates.

**The finding that matters most: this box is almost entirely Volume 1 material.** Seventy-one cards land in Volume 1 and four in Volume 2 — one Threshold, one Companions, two Quiet Familiarity. Per §2, Volume 1 is structurally closed and Volume 2 is where active curation happens, so a reserve pool this lopsided doesn't help where help is wanted. It's worth knowing before the next buying trip: Volume 2 needs domestic scale, bonds, liminal edges, and self-contained stillness, and this box supplied almost none of it.

**Intimidation overload — resolved.** It initially took 11 cards, which §1's "no theme may function as a dumping ground" makes a structural problem rather than a cosmetic one. Genesect and the HGSS Houndour have been moved to Contemplation: both read as watchful rather than menacing, and Contemplation was thin at three. That leaves Intimidation at 9 and Contemplation at 5, and no further action is needed. (The alternatives considered — splitting Intimidation into two spreads, or holding Gengar-line cards back — are unnecessary now, but remain available if the theme grows again.)

**On the Attack at ten** is a second-order effect of applying §5A honestly: Torterra, Charizard VSTAR, and Groudon all moved out of the Power Awakening chapter once "release, not gathering" was enforced. That is the rule working as intended, but it's worth checking the resulting spread doesn't become a wall of undifferentiated impact.
