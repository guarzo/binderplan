## 5. New Theme Analysis

### Themes Considered and Rejected

**"The Vast" / Scale (carried from January):**
Still insufficient evidence. A few cards in reserve show vast environments but they distribute to Elemental Solitude or Contemplation. Hold for observation.

**"Guardians" or "Watchers":**
Would capture Pokémon in protective/watchful poses. Rejected — cannibalizes Legendary Bearing (contained power watching) and Contemplation (watchful stillness). Not a distinct axis.

**"Hound Pack" / Species Collection:**
The Houndour/Houndoom concentration is a collecting impulse, not a curatorial one. Reject firmly.

**"Warmth/Firelight":**
Concentration exists in reserve: Light Arcanine, Vulpix, Charmander variants, Eevee near fire. But "warmth" is a visual quality, not a distinct emotional axis. These cards distribute correctly to Quiet Familiarity (domestic warmth) and Calm in Nature (at peace with element). Rejected.

**"Cosmic/Otherworldly":**
Deoxys (x2), Arceus/Dialga/Palkia. Too few cards, and they distribute cleanly to Enduring Presence and Threshold based on actual signal. Rejected.

### Theme Recommendation

**No new themes recommended.** The Volume 2 restructure to 3 chapters was the right move. The problem remains execution, not architecture. The reserve pool, even after deep review, distributes to existing themes without leaving orphans that demand a new axis. Revisit only after:
1. Enduring Presence contains 1+ complete spread
2. Threshold contains 1+ complete spread
3. A genuine classification problem emerges from curation practice

## 6. Slab Recategorization (March 2026)

### Problem

Crown Art was bloated (14 cards — nearly half the total slabs) while Historical was anemic (3 cards). Several cards were miscategorized:

- **Masaki promos** (Golem, Alakazam, Gengar) were in Crown Art but their significance is *historical/cultural*, not pinnacle art
- **Pikachu Black Star 2000** was in Definitive but its weight is *era-defining*, not "the definitive Pikachu"
- **Charizard 151 JP** was in Definitive alongside **Charizard Base Set 2** — two Charizards in "the single best card for a species" violates the category's own rule
- **Four Gempack placeholders** (Umbreon, Leafeon, Eevee, Sylveon) had no real images and inflated Crown Art with unfinished entries

### Moves Executed

| Card | From | To | Reasoning |
|------|------|----|-----------|
| Golem Masaki | Crown Art | Historical | Cultural artifact, not pinnacle art |
| Alakazam Masaki | Crown Art | Historical | Cultural artifact, not pinnacle art |
| Gengar Masaki | Crown Art | Historical | Cultural artifact, not pinnacle art |
| Pikachu Black Star 2000 | Definitive | Historical | Era-defining promo, not "the definitive Pikachu" |
| Charizard 151 JP | Definitive | Crown Art | The 151 SAR is celebrated for art; resolves two-Charizard conflict |
| Umbreon Gempack | Crown Art | Removed | Placeholder — no real image, pending grading |
| Leafeon Gempack | Crown Art | Removed | Placeholder — no real image, pending grading |
| Eevee Gempack | Crown Art | Removed | Placeholder — no real image, pending grading |
| Sylveon Gempack | Crown Art | Removed | Placeholder — no real image, pending grading |

### Result

| Category | Before | After | Notes |
|----------|--------|-------|-------|
| Crown Art | 14 | 8 | Focused on actual artistic excellence |
| Definitive | 10 | 8 | Each species appears exactly once |
| Historical | 3 | 7 | Masaki promos + Black Star land where they belong |
| Personal | 5 | 5 | Unchanged — already solid |

### Pending

- **Gempack Eeveelutions** — Re-add once graded with real images. Categorize by actual signal: Crown Art if the art justifies it, Definitive if each is the best card for its species
- **LIQUID category** — Defined in grading philosophy but absent from gallery and themes. Decide: is this intentional (investment slabs aren't displayed) or an oversight?

## Closing Note

The 3-chapter restructure of Volume 2 was the right architectural decision. But architecture without execution creates a worse state than what preceded it — Era Identity at least had cards. The binder now has a tighter conceptual framework wrapped around more empty space.

The January audit's card-level recommendations were sound. They remain sound. The work is not analytical — it is physical. Cards need to move.

**Priority order:**
1. Dissolve Era Identity (resolve the limbo)
2. Populate Enduring Presence (eliminate the critical gap)
3. Execute Legendary Bearing extractions (restore thematic clarity)
4. Complete Quiet Familiarity 2 (close the structural hole)
5. Begin Threshold curation (accept that this will be slow)

**The goal is not to grow the binder quickly — the goal is to preserve its coherence over time.**
