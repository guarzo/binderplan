# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
People who love Pokemon cards as visual art, plus the curator himself. Visitors arrive out of curiosity or shared obsession, browsing on a laptop or phone in a relaxed, unhurried moment. They are not buying, grading, or pricing anything. They came to look, to feel the mood of a page, and to understand how one collector thinks about cards as a body of work.

## Product Purpose
A personal, exhibition-style home for a Pokemon card collection curated by emotion and narrative rather than set, rarity, or value. The binder spreads are photographed as complete compositions and meant to be read like pages in an art book. Success is when a visitor stops scrolling, looks closely, and comes away feeling they walked through a small, opinionated museum rather than a card database.

## Brand Personality
Reverent, intimate, quietly obsessive. Voice is first-person, warm, and confident without being precious. Three words: curatorial, luminous, sincere. The interface should evoke the hush of a gallery and the shimmer of a foil card catching light.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS / dashboard chrome (dark-blue, uniform cards, sticky app header). This is what it must move away from.
- TCG marketplaces (TCGPlayer, eBay): price tags, condition badges, buy buttons, listing-grid clutter.
- Crypto / NFT galleries: neon-on-black, glossy 3D, hype energy.
- Corporate-minimal startup landing pages: sterile white, stock-photo cleanliness.

## Design Principles
- **The image is the subject.** Chrome recedes; the photographed spreads carry the page. Type and layout frame them, never compete.
- **Curated, not catalogued.** Composition, sequence, and whitespace communicate that a human chose this order for a reason.
- **Luminous, not loud.** Borrow the shimmer of foil as atmosphere (soft light, restrained iridescence), never as decoration for its own sake.
- **Read like a book.** Volumes, chapters, and themes should feel like turning pages, with rhythm and pacing rather than a uniform grid.
- **Earned stillness.** Generous negative space and slow, exponential motion signal confidence and let the work breathe.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Dark, low-glare presentation appropriate for relaxed viewing. Maintain WCAG AA contrast for all text over backgrounds and image overlays. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`. Do not rely on color alone to convey structure. Keep the lightbox keyboard-operable (Escape to close, focusable trigger).
