# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
People who love Pokémon cards as visual art, plus the curator himself. Visitors arrive out of curiosity or shared obsession, browsing on a laptop or phone in a relaxed, unhurried moment. Most come to look, not shop: they want to feel the mood of a page and understand how one collector thinks about cards as a body of work. A small number may also follow a shared link to the five still-owned Holding cards explicitly available for trade; the site is not a priced storefront.

## Product Purpose
A personal, exhibition-style home for a Pokémon card collection curated by emotion and narrative rather than set, rarity, or value. Volumes I and II, Stamped Cards, the four-pocket Emolga Masterset, and Holding use local-image digital binder presentations. The photographed Holding pages preserve observed physical order; its five top-loaded Trade cards have a separate digital page, not invented pockets. Trainer Full Arts is a four-page digital sequence of 29 Trainer entries in the owner's PDF, not a claim about photographed physical pocket order. Slab-based collections use a distinct whole-object presentation. Success is when a visitor stops scrolling, looks closely, and comes away feeling they walked through a small, opinionated museum rather than a card database.

## Brand Personality
Reverent, intimate, quietly obsessive. Voice is first-person, warm, and confident without being precious. Three words: curatorial, luminous, sincere. The interface should evoke the hush of a gallery and the shimmer of a foil card catching light.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS / dashboard chrome (dark-blue, uniform cards, sticky app header). This is what it must move away from.
- TCG marketplaces (TCGPlayer, eBay): price tags, condition badges, buy buttons, listing-grid clutter.
- Crypto / NFT galleries: neon-on-black, glossy 3D, hype energy.
- Corporate-minimal startup landing pages: sterile white, stock-photo cleanliness.

## Design Principles
- **The image is the subject.** Chrome recedes; reviewed local card images and slab photographs carry each page. Type and layout frame them, never compete.
- **Curated, not catalogued.** Composition, sequence, and whitespace communicate that a human chose this order for a reason.
- **Luminous, not loud.** Borrow the shimmer of foil as atmosphere (soft light, restrained iridescence), never as decoration for its own sake.
- **Read like a book.** Volumes, chapters, and themes should feel like turning pages, with rhythm and pacing rather than a uniform grid.
- **Earned stillness.** Generous negative space and slow, exponential motion signal confidence and let the work breathe.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Dark, low-glare presentation appropriate for relaxed viewing. Maintain WCAG AA contrast for all text over backgrounds and image overlays. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`. Do not rely on color alone to convey structure. Keep binder and slab inspectors keyboard-operable (Escape to close, focusable trigger). Keep uncertainty and provenance available in the inspector and source records while physical checks remain open; do not present a catalogue image as verified in-hand printing.
