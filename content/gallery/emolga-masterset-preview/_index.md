This is a **draft**, not the public gallery. The pages follow the binder photographs from left to right, top to bottom. Pictured owned cards use local TCGdex or DoubleHolo catalogue images where the artwork and printed number match. **Catalog scan** means the image is a marked proxy: the card is pictured in the binder, but its exact foil or edition may differ. Printings without a trustworthy provider image keep an honest crop of the archived pocket photograph. The two unresolved identities are marked separately.

The two reference prints remain in their photographed pockets, clearly marked **not owned**. The actual wanted printings appear separately below the binder. The [published photo gallery](/gallery/emolga-masterset/) has not changed.
