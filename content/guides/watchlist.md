## Active Replacement Targets

These solve known weaknesses in existing Volume II pages.

### Quiet Familiarity — Home Life

- Pokemon sharing a kitchen, meal, or food preparation
- Pokemon sleeping or waking in an established bedroom or living space
- Pokemon among books, toys, laundry, dishes, or household clutter
- A child, elder, or family caring for a Pokemon without dramatic emotion
- Pokemon participating in a quiet evening or morning ritual

### Quiet Familiarity — Shared Routine

- Pokemon helping in a shop, workshop, farm, school, or workplace
- Gardening, watering, harvesting, sweeping, carrying, or delivering
- A repeated neighborhood route, commute, or familiar form of transport
- Pokemon recognized as a regular part of a community
- A public scene made intimate through repetition rather than spectacle

### Threshold — Built Boundaries

- Doorway with visible inside and outside
- Bridge or stairs with a clear destination
- Train, station, platform, tracks, or departure gate
- Window, tunnel, archway, fence opening, or city gate
- Pokemon hesitating before or looking back after crossing

### Threshold — Natural Boundaries

- Shoreline dividing land and water
- Cave mouth dividing light and darkness
- Forest edge opening into field, road, town, or sky
- Cliff, ridge, or horizon with a destination beyond it
- Dawn, dusk, storm edge, snowfall, or seasonal transition used as a boundary

### Enduring Presence — Preserved

- Fossil Pokemon presented as recovered history rather than active fighters
- Pokemon beside ruins, shrines, monuments, or archaeological remains
- Archival, museum, specimen, photograph, or artifact framing
- Ancient machinery or objects whose age matters to the scene
- A sleeping, sealed, buried, or rediscovered presence

### Enduring Presence — Remaining

- Pokemon returning across generations or being remembered by different people
- A town, culture, or landscape visibly formed around a Pokemon
- Weathered guardians and old beings integrated into an established place
- Modern life continuing beneath or around something ancient
- Quiet scale that makes human time feel brief

## Future Theme Candidate Pools

Do not build these themes yet. Place possible cards in labeled reserve sections and promote a theme only when 6–9 cards pass its complete test.

### Journey

**Core signal:** purposeful travel toward a readable destination.

**Shop for:**
- Long paths, roads, tracks, rivers, or routes extending through the image
- Pokemon carrying supplies, accompanying travelers, or moving in a group
- Rest stops, maps, distant towns, mountain passes, and changing terrain
- Departure with anticipation rather than sadness
- Scale that makes the route feel longer than the immediate action

**Reject:** generic running, flying, attacks, wandering without direction, or a single Threshold crossing.

**Possible nine-pocket structure:** departure → road → distance.

### Aftermath

**Core signal:** the action is over, but its physical or emotional consequences remain.

**Shop for:**
- Exhausted Pokemon after battle or effort
- Broken ground, smoke, rain, debris, empty arenas, or settling dust
- Recovery, tending wounds, regrouping, or returning home
- A victor shown without triumph
- Silence immediately following danger or transformation

**Reject:** active attacks, power gathering, ordinary sleep, generic sadness, or pristine dramatic poses.

**Possible nine-pocket structure:** impact remembered → recovery → quiet resolution.

### Melancholy

**Core signal:** sadness, absence, or emotional distance is the subject of the image.

**Shop for:**
- A lone Pokemon in rain, an empty room, or a place meant for more than one
- Waiting for someone who is absent
- Abandoned objects, fading memories, separation, or unreachable company
- Downcast posture combined with environmental reinforcement
- Muted distance rather than immediate fear or danger

**Reject:** neutral solitude, contemplation, ordinary rain, sleep, cuteness, or unease without sadness.

**Possible nine-pocket structure:** absence → waiting → acceptance.

## Observation Only

Keep these as notes, not shopping missions:

- **Defiance:** promising, but likely to overlap Intimidation and On the Attack
- **Unease:** promising, but requires a clearer distinction from Intimidation
- **Dreamlike:** currently too stylistic and likely to cannibalize every volume
- **Monochrome Mood:** a page-composition tool, not a narrative theme
- **Perspective Play:** an art-study category, not yet part of the binder's emotional language

Card:

If the target page or replacement is blank, the card is probably admiration rather than acquisition.
