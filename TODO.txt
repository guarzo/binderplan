2. REORGANIZE THE STAMPED CARD BINDER
-------------------------------------

Goal:
Replace the current sequence of numbered spreads with an organization based on
why and where each stamp was issued.

Proposed sections:
- Prerelease and Build & Battle
- League and tournament
- Set-logo and era-specific reverse holos
- Retail, event, and location exclusives
- Crossovers and promotional campaigns

Within each section:
- Order chronologically.
- Keep visually related stamp treatments together where practical.
- Add section labels or divider cards.
- Document unclear stamp provenance before assigning a permanent location.

Goal:
Make every card findable and make binder changes traceable without turning the

Possible format:
- Start with CSV, YAML, or JSON only when maintaining it feels worthwhile.

Suggested fields:
- Card name
- Set and collector number
- Language and variant
- Collection, volume, theme, page, and pocket
- Status: binder, slab, holding box, candidate, duplicate, or outgoing
- Condition
- Acquisition date and cost
- Replacement target
- Curatorial notes

Possible location ID:
- V2-NEAR-COMP-02-B2

Before implementing:

- Decide how much purchase and condition data should remain private.
